Natural stones whose Italian name starts with D-L, among those most often used in jewellery. For a short guide to the most common ones see [Stones]({% include url-ref.html ref="pietre" %}).

- Dumortierite
- Fluorite
- Garnet
- Hematite
- Howlite
- Iolite
- Jade
- Jasper
- Kunzite
- Labradorite
- Lapis lazuli
- Larimar
- Lepidolite
