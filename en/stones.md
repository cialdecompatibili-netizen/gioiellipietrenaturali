Some of the natural stones most often used in jewellery. The list will grow: if you are looking for a stone that is not here, ask us.

- **Amethyst**: purple quartz, one of the most common stones in jewellery.
- **Rose quartz**: quartz of a soft pink, often chosen for delicate jewellery.
- **Tiger's eye**: a golden-brown stone with shimmering reflections.
- **Lapis lazuli**: a deep blue stone, used for ornaments for centuries.
- **Jade**: a green stone, widely used in jewellery and crafts.
- **Obsidian**: dark volcanic glass of natural origin.
- **Agate**: banded quartz, in many colours and patterns.
- **Labradorite**: a blue-grey stone with iridescent reflections.

Colour and pattern vary from stone to stone, because they are natural materials: two pieces are never exactly the same. Some stones are softer and need more care: see the guide on [how to care for stone jewellery]({% include url-ref.html ref="cura" %}).

Meanings attributed to stones belong to tradition and are not scientifically proven: read [what is said and what is not]({% include url-ref.html ref="tradizione" %}).
