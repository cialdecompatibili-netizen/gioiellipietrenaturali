For each intention, the stones that **tradition** associates with it. These are symbolic and cultural meanings: they are not scientifically proven and do not replace medical care. Read also [what is said and what is not]({% include url-ref.html ref="tradizione" %}).

## Love {#amore}

rose quartz, rhodonite, rhodochrosite.

## Protection {#protezione}

tiger's eye, black obsidian, onyx.

## Calm {#calma}

amethyst, agate, amazonite.

## Communication {#comunicazione}

sodalite, aquamarine, lapis lazuli.

## Self-confidence {#fiducia}

tiger's eye, citrine, carnelian.

## Study and exams {#studio}

fluorite, lapis lazuli, sodalite.

## Meditation and focus {#meditazione}

amethyst, labradorite, smoky quartz.

## Intuition {#intuizione}

amethyst, labradorite, moonstone.

## Energy {#energia}

carnelian, citrine, garnet.
