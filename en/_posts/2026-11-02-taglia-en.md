A well-chosen bracelet neither pinches nor slides off. All you need is one measurement.

## How to measure your wrist

1. Take a tape measure or a strip of paper.
2. Wrap it around your wrist, just below the bone.
3. Mark the point and measure the length.

## How much to add

You generally add one or two centimetres to the wrist measurement, depending on whether you like it snug or loose. Bracelets with rigid beads have less margin than those on a cord.

## Adjustable models

Bracelets with an adjustable clasp fit several wrists and are handy as a gift, when you do not know the size.

## For children

Wrists grow: an adjustable model is better, with a check every now and then.
