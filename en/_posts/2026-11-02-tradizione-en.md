Over the centuries meanings and symbols have been handed down around stones: love, protection, calm, courage. They are part of **tradition** and popular culture.

## What is proven and what is not

- That a stone has a certain **colour, hardness or origin** is a fact that can be checked.
- That a stone has **effects on health or fate** is not scientifically proven.

For this reason jewellery never replaces medical treatment or the advice of a professional.

## How to choose

You can choose a stone because you like it, because it reminds you of someone or because it has a symbolic meaning for you: it is a lovely, personal way to choose jewellery, as long as it is clear that the value is symbolic.
