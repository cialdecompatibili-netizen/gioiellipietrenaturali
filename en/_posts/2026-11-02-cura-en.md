Natural stones are resistant but not indestructible. Some are softer or more porous than others, so it pays to be careful.

## Cleaning

- Use a soft cloth, dry or just slightly damp.
- Avoid harsh detergents and chemicals.
- Dry well before putting the piece away.

## What to avoid

- Perfume, creams and hairspray: put them on before wearing the piece.
- Knocks and falls, which can chip stones.
- Strong heat and long exposure to direct sun, which can alter some colours.

## Storage

Keep pieces separate, in a box or a soft pouch, so they do not scratch each other. Take off cord bracelets before showering, swimming in the sea or in a pool.

If you are unsure about a stone, ask the seller for care instructions.
