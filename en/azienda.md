Natural stone jewellery for women, men, couples and children, with guides to get to know each stone.

## The founder

The project grew out of its founder's idea of bringing together two things that rarely go hand in hand: the beauty of natural stones and quality that lasts. Every piece is chosen and offered with this in mind: a piece of jewellery should please you on the first day and keep pleasing you years later.

## Our goal

**Jewellery that lasts.** That is why we set our stones in **316L stainless steel**, the most widely used material in good-quality fashion jewellery: it is strong, does not rust, does not tarnish, keeps its shine over time and is well tolerated by most skin types. You can wear it every day without a second thought.

The stones are natural: each piece may show slight differences in colour and veining, which is exactly what makes it unique. Handmade.
