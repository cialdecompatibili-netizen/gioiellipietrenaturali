In the tradition of Indian origin the **chakras** are seven energy centres of the body, each linked to a colour. The stones listed are those **traditionally associated**: it is a symbolic custom, not a scientifically proven practice.

1. **Root** (red): garnet, red jasper.
2. **Sacral** (orange): carnelian.
3. **Solar plexus** (yellow): citrine, tiger's eye.
4. **Heart** (green): aventurine, rose quartz.
5. **Throat** (light blue): sodalite, aquamarine.
6. **Third eye** (indigo): lapis lazuli, amethyst.
7. **Crown** (violet): amethyst, rock crystal.
